Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about your upcoming dental visit? Here are answers to common inquiries from our patients.

We recommend a comprehensive dental check-up and professional teeth cleaning every six months. Regular visits allow our dentists to detect early enamel demineralization, remove hardened tartar that brushing cannot dislodge, and catch gum issues before they turn into complex conditions.
Modern root canal treatment is designed to relieve pain, not cause it. With advanced local anesthetics, motorized rotary endomotors, and gentle file systems, the procedure is carried out with utmost care for your comfort. Most patients report that it feels quite similar to having a routine filling placed.
Teeth whitening results typically last anywhere from 6 months to over a year, depending significantly on your dietary habits (such as consumption of tea, coffee, turmeric, or tobacco) and your daily oral hygiene routine. We also provide guidance on maintaining your brightened shade for as long as possible.
Most adults with one or more missing teeth and healthy gums are good candidates for dental implants. Sufficient jawbone density is important to anchor the titanium implant securely. During your consultation, we evaluate your bone structure with digital imaging to determine the most predictable restorative approach.
If you suffer from sudden severe toothache, a broken tooth, facial swelling, or dental trauma, call our clinic desk immediately at +91 99909 19344. We will advise you on immediate first-aid steps over the phone and coordinate a priority consultation slot during clinic hours.
When you submit your enquiry through our website form, it instantly compiles your details into a structured message and connects you to our clinic desk via WhatsApp or direct phone. Our team will verify doctor availability and confirm your preferred slot promptly.
